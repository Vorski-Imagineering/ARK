Create one copy per participating organisation only after the organisation has agreed to take part.

Suggested filename: `source-packs/<approved-public-organisation-slug>.md`.

## Public-repository safety

This repository is public. Complete this pack using only information already public or explicitly approved for publication.

Do not include:

- Private participant names or personal contact details.
- Private conversations or quotations.
- Internal URLs, meeting links, ticket identifiers, or access instructions.
- Credentials, tokens, passwords, private keys, or environment values.
- Confidential commercial, financial, contractual, or roadmap information.
- Unpublished personal, organisational, or member data.
- Sources the organisation does not have the right to share or authorise for this use.

Nominate people and exchange contact details privately with the event lead. In this file, use public role labels such as “organisation representative” or “technical contributor” unless publication of an identity has been explicitly approved.

If a source or answer would be inappropriate on a public website, do not include it.

## Agent-assisted completion

A small working ARC Agent should be available during the two-week ramp-up. It may help complete and review this pack by:

- Explaining each field from the public event documents.
- Checking whether required fields are missing.
- Testing whether public URLs are accessible.
- Suggesting a shorter public profile based on approved public material.
- Drafting representative questions and identifying likely cross-organisation themes.
- Flagging an unclear permission statement for human review.

The organisation representative must approve the final profile, sources, permission statement, and representative questions. Agent output is a draft, not organisational approval. Do not give the agent private names, contact details, meeting transcripts, credentials, or non-public information while completing this pack.

## Completion test

This pack is complete when:

1. The organisation has made an explicit participation commitment.
2. Attendance coverage is credible.
3. A concise public profile and one to three usable sources are present.
4. Permission and limitations are explicit.
5. Desired value and at least one representative question are stated.
6. The organiser has tested access and passed the public-information check.

An organisation is not counted toward the commitment gate until this completion test passes.
