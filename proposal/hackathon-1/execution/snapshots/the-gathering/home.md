Why Gather

Humanity is in the depths of a meta-poly-meaning crisis, the only path forward is through a great transformation.

A wave of visionary people, organisations and movements is emerging to bring forth this transformation and to embrace the opportunities presented by the challenges. Human ecosystems need gatherings to nourish them, we come together to build connection, relationships, and collaborations. The Gathering is a festival, a conference, a fair, a university and an accelerator for companies, communities, villages, tribes, DAOs. A coming together of every kind of organisation, formal and informal, dedicated to systems change, to building a better future for all, and to understanding what “better” means...

A new gathering for a new era

Co-creation

The Gathering is an invitation to contribute. Co-created by all participants, The Gathering is a space nurturing creativity and welcoming wide-scale contribution. A “No Spectators” event, The Gathering asks all participants to put intention into their presence, to ask the question how they are contributing to the event and to the ecosystem. For example in a grand bi-annual Gathering co-creation takes on the form of a co-created program for each camp, invitation to pre events online, and a wide invitation to volunteer.

The Gathering invites no spectators

Action

The Gathering is a serendipity catalyst, a high-energy collisional space to get inspired, to learn but especially to build new relationships, to connect for action, to find opportunities for collaboration. Whether to find teachers, build community, team or a project. The Gathering is a rich ecosystem, a supportive medium to grow connections and move together into action. The Gathering brings together the people you need. The Gathering invites us to ask “what’s the next best thing we can do together”?

The Gathering fosters a culture of connect-for-action

Nature

Rethinking and rebuilding our relationship to nature is necessary for deep systems change, it is at the core of regeneration. To support this reconnection, The Gathering is a festival of communal connection with nature. The Gathering invites nature as a primary participant in the co-creation. In nature we feel connected to our roots, we are calmer and more creative, outdoor spaces invite spontaneous interaction, conversation flows, deep connections are made with ease.

The Gathering invites  nature as host and guide

Weaving

The Gathering brings together systems change movements. Weaving together the whole spectrum of organisations seeking to re-imagine and re-invent our societal systems, the movements re-thinking life, work, our fundamental beliefs and societal patterns.

The Gathering weaves systems change movements

Celebration

The Gathering is a healthy, regenerative experience, a path to deep and meaningful relationships this requires a balance of being and doing. We understand celebration to include a all “being” activities, for example: wellness, art, music, mindfulness, dancing, singing, circling.

The Gathering is a celebration

Belonging

The Gathering is an inquiry into belonging - who is my tribe? What ecosystems am I contributing to? By participating organisations explore and strengthen their identity, understanding and connecting with their ecosystem. The Gathering invites organisations and individuals to ask: “How are we nurtured and how do we contribute to our ecosystem?

The Gathering asks  where do you belong?

DNA

A Gathering is created by expressing each of the six interconnected patterns forming our DNA.

Codifying The Gathering as DNA of patterns rather than rules allows every edition of The Gathering to share a core essence whilst being a rich expression of local culture, answering local needs.

The Gathering is an action-learning prototype for “regenerative scaling” a search for a model how to globally spread regenerative events, projects, organisation and business models.

Co-Created Programme

Each participant is a co-creator of The Gathering.

The Gathering is an invitation to think what you want to contribute, to the world, to your bioregion and neighbourhood, and to express this in The Gathering. Everything is welcome workshops, music, dance, motion, questions, art, music, a shop... What do you think The Gathering needs, where is your joy in contribution? Your imagination is the only limit! Share, anything that enables you to show and explore why, who, how and what your community, your organisation, your movement, your tribe, is about. Share questions as much as answers, ask for help, as much as offering it. Connect with people, organisations, tribes, movements, ideas, questions and answers.

In Nature

The Gathering takes brings us together in an immersive experience in nature. For 5-7 days we camp together living in the dream society.

Amenities provided by The Gathering include: parking, community kitchens, showers and bathrooms, access to drinking water, (limited) electricity, indoors spaces, campsite, glamping (at extra cost), a lake to swim in, a forest to walk through and much to explore. (details vary depending on local conditions)
