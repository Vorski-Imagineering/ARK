# 09. Governance, Privacy, And Trust

## Purpose

Define the rules that keep guide agents safe, reviewable, and properly scoped.

## Topics to answer

- What categories of data are allowed into the memory plane.
- What must be redacted, anonymized, encrypted, or excluded before ingestion.
- What the visibility scopes are for participant, cohort, operator, and agent memories.
- What trust model applies to cross-agent messages and group-memory proposals.
- Which writes require human review.
- What approval, rollback, and deletion guarantees are mandatory.
- How provenance, signed writes, and policy decisions are recorded.
- What the policy is for sharing or publishing dream reports or summaries externally.

## Included answers from current materials

- Multi-agent coordination must not collapse privacy, trust, or provenance.
- Humans must be able to review, override, rollback, and audit the system.
- One open question is which outputs need human approval by default: memory promotion, summaries, recommendations, or external messages.
- One open question is how much shared memory is safe in v1 before trust and privacy boundaries are mature enough.

## Still to answer

- The actual review and approval policy.
- The visibility and trust model across scopes.
- The deletion, redaction, and rollback guarantees.
