# 06. Dreaming And Consolidation Architecture

## Purpose

Define how evidence is consolidated into durable memory and what `dreaming` means in this system.

## Topics to answer

- What counts as a `dream` in this system.
- The promotion rules from episodic evidence into durable memory.
- What candidate types can be promoted.
- What must always be verified before promotion.
- The difference between local dreams and group dreams.
- The cadence for micro-dreams, nightly deep dreams, and group dreams.
- Which dream outputs are explanatory only and must never be treated as truth.
- What rollback and reindex mechanisms exist if a dream writes something wrong.

## Included answers from current materials

- `Dreaming` should mean governed periodic consolidation over evidence, not free-form memory promotion.
- One open question is what the smallest pilot is that proves the architecture without prematurely committing to full group-memory complexity.
