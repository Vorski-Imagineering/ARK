# 12. Migration And Implementation Sequencing

## Purpose

Define the order of work needed to turn the current direction into a buildable METIS platform.

## Topics to answer

- What can be reused from the current Genesis Brain pipeline without major redesign.
- What must be built first to de-risk the architecture.
- What the minimum end-to-end slice is that proves the design.
- What the smallest slice is that proves easy agent setup in METIS rather than only one successful custom agent.
- What should be explicitly deferred from v1.
- What data migrations or backfills are needed.
- What experiments should happen before schema lock-in.
- What the phase plan is from local dreams to governed group memory.

## Included answers from current materials

- A major open question is the smallest pilot that proves the architecture without prematurely committing to full group-memory complexity.
- The design process should move from research questions to implementation only after the core architectural decisions are answered.
